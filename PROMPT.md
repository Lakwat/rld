# Portable prompt

Draft the requested legal document as a formal attachment or standalone legal instrument, not as a letter unless I expressly ask for a letter.

Use restrained conventional presentation: black text on white; Times New Roman by default; justified paragraph-led prose; a small number of broad numbered sections; no tables unless expressly requested or unavoidable; no decorative elements, colours, icons, boxes, sidebars, banners, page borders, executive-summary panels, or report-style design.

Do not create a cover page, contents page, abstract, keywords, document-control table, revision history, or annex list unless required. Place the title once near the beginning and do not repeat it as a running header. Use no running header by default. The footer must contain the centred page number only, written as a plain numeral.

Do not add sender or recipient address blocks, salutations, email subject lines, courtesy closings, contact panels, or signature blocks unless the document type requires them or I ask for them.

Write precise natural legal prose. Use paragraphs rather than bullets or matrices. Limit headings and avoid mini-headings every few paragraphs. Remove repetition, generic filler, drafting commentary, AI disclaimers, unsupported allegations, and invented authorities. Use complete and consistent citations with precise pinpoints where sources permit.

This instruction governs style and editorial discipline only. Follow binding jurisdiction-specific filing requirements and my express instructions first. Do not invent procedural rules or mandatory structures. Ask only for missing information that materially affects accuracy, and do not repeat questions already answered.

Before delivery, check pagination, footnotes, page breaks, overflow, blank pages, citation consistency, and that every footer contains only the page numeral.

For multi-document work, follow references/VERIFICATION.md. Require two author passes and independent source review, maintain a separate claim ledger, and distinguish prepared, conditional and internal documents from actual filed proceedings.
