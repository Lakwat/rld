# Conformance review

Use this checklist after generating or editing the final document.

## Substance

1. Every factual allegation is supported by the user's materials or clearly identified as an assumption outside the document.
2. Every legal proposition that required research is grounded in an authoritative and current source.
3. Quotations, dates, case numbers, statutory references, paragraph numbers, exhibits, and monetary amounts have been checked against the source.
4. The relief, request, conclusion, or action sought is unambiguous.
5. No paragraph merely repeats an earlier point in different words.

## Structure

1. The document uses a limited number of broad numbered sections.
2. The main body is continuous prose. Numbered paragraphs are acceptable; tables and bullet lists are absent unless specifically justified.
3. The title appears once.
4. There is no automatic cover, contents page, executive summary, abstract, keyword list, revision table, or decorative annex index.
5. The document does not imitate an email or business letter unless that is the requested form.

## Typography and page furniture

1. All text and rules are black on white.
2. The typeface is Times New Roman unless overridden.
3. Body text is justified and spacing is restrained.
4. Headings are functional, not decorative, and do not occur every few paragraphs.
5. There is no running header.
6. Every footer contains only one centred Arabic numeral.
7. No page contains a logo, icon, colour block, sidebar, callout, banner, decorative border, or report-style panel.

## Technical output

1. No text is clipped, overlapping, orphaned inappropriately, or outside the printable area.
2. Footnotes remain on the correct page and use consistent numbering.
3. Page breaks do not create empty pages or isolated headings.
4. PDF text is selectable where possible and fonts render consistently.
5. The filename is neutral and professional.
6. Drafting notes, source commentary, uncertainties, and explanations are delivered separately from the formal document.

## Release integrity

1. The author completed and recorded a source pass and adversarial pass.
2. An independent reviewer examined the substantive claims and requested corrections where needed.
3. Each instrument has a stable ID and a separate source ledger.
4. Prepared, conditional and internal statuses reflect actual remaining work.
5. Filing status is supported by a registration receipt; drafting is never recorded as submission.
6. Every page was visually inspected after its final content change.
