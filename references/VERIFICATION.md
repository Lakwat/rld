# Source verification and release gates

Use this extension for document packs that depend on legal authorities and developing evidence.

1. Assign a stable document ID and a single author. Record purpose, recipient, language, jurisdiction, reference period and filing route before drafting.
2. Maintain a separate claim ledger. For every material legal, factual or procedural proposition, record the exact authoritative URL, provision/page, date checked, source date, supporting passage or faithful paraphrase and qualification. A roadmap, search snippet or another agent's answer is a lead, not verification.
3. Read the operative source. Check amendments, applicability dates, exceptions, duty holders and the exact statutory trigger. Distinguish publication date from coverage period. Never turn absence of a located record into proof of absence of performance.
4. Conduct two author passes: first source-led drafting; then an adversarial pass testing counter-evidence, exceptions, missing facts, attribution, remedies, timing and overbroad requests. Record actual corrections and unresolved points.
5. Require a separate reviewer to inspect the draft and primary sources. Return material defects to the author; record the response. Self-review does not constitute independent approval.
6. Use truthful statuses: prepared means text ready for applicant particulars and verified filing channel; conditional means evidence/procedural events remain; internal means not for submission. None means signed, submitted, received, accepted or legally successful.
7. For access requests, seek pre-existing information, identify the custodian, narrow dates, request native outputs where held, and offer partial access and established outputs before burden-heavy extraction. Request referral under the correct regime. Do not silently conflate State and autonomous-community law.
8. For preservation, request proportionate lawful retention rather than asserting a private litigation hold, compulsory discovery or a freeze of all deletion. Minimise personal data.
9. For allegations, separate substantiated facts, hypotheses, contrary evidence and gaps. Identify each duty holder. A disruption alone is not a passenger-rights infringement. Do not fill evidential placeholders with invented events.
10. Calendar a deadline only after verifying its trigger, service/receipt, competent recipient, month-versus-day rule, holidays and suspensions. Silence, explicit refusal and failure to deliver may have different review rules.
11. Keep identity, signatures, evidence and working case files out of public code repositories. Publish only reusable tools and non-personal methods unless expressly authorised otherwise.
12. Render each final document, inspect every page, and correct clipping, isolated headings, blank pages and footers. Re-check edited outputs. Preserve original source materials separately and hash released files.
13. Record the exact release gates in a separate document register. Conditional templates must be unmistakably labelled in their filenames and register; they cannot be counted as filed or factually completed.
